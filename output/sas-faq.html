Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About SAS.

Everything you need to know about working with Strategic Assistant Staffing.

  • How quickly can I hire a Virtual Assistant?

    Most clients are matched with qualified candidates within 7–10 days. Our team handles sourcing, screening, and vetting so you only interview top candidates.

  • What tasks can your Virtual Assistants handle?

    Our Virtual Assistants support executive assistance, marketing, social media management, customer service, sales support, reporting, research, and general operations.

  • How do you ensure the quality of your Virtual Assistants?

    Every Virtual Assistant goes through multiple interviews, skill testing, and cultural evaluation. Only the top performers are selected to join our team.

  • Do I manage the Virtual Assistant myself?

    You work directly with your Virtual Assistant, while SAS provides a dedicated Account Manager and ongoing coaching to support long-term success and growth.

  • How do you handle security?

    All Virtual Assistants sign strict confidentiality agreements and follow professional security protocols to help ensure your data and systems remain protected.

  • Will the Virtual Assistant work in my time zone?

    Yes. Our Virtual Assistants can adjust their schedules to align with your business hours, supporting real-time collaboration and seamless communication.
